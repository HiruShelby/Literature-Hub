Exam Strategy

How to Write High-Scoring O/L Answers

Examiners look for clear textual evidence and analytical explanation rather than mere plot retelling. Use these two proven frameworks.

I

Mastering Context Questions

Paper I • Part A (Identifying Extracts)

When given a short quote from any syllabus text, ensure your answer covers these 4 essential checkpoints:

1

Title & Author

State the exact title (in quotation marks or underlined) and author's full name.

2

Speaker & Listener

Identify who speaks these words and to whom (or narrator's inner thought).

3

Immediate Situation

Briefly explain what is happening at that exact moment in the plot.

4

Literary Significance

Explain what the quote reveals about character, theme, tone, or technique.

II

The P.E.E.L. Essay Method

Paper II • Literary Appreciation Essays

Structure every body paragraph in your essays using P.E.E.L. to stay focused on the question:

P — Point: Begin with a clear topic sentence answering the prompt.
E — Evidence: Embed a short, accurate quotation or specific textual detail.
E — Explanation: Analyze how the language, imagery, or action creates meaning.
L — Link: Connect your insight back to the main theme or prompt.
